v19.7 · Nov 26, 2026 — round flashlight

  • The flashlight beam is a clean circle again at every distance. Past a few metres it had been worked out on the coarser shading pixels used for distant lighting, which turned the edge of the beam into a staircase of squares; the flashlight now always uses the fine block pixels

v19.6 · Nov 26, 2026 — why it got faster over time, and steadier from the start

  • The world’s ~26,000 objects (a parked car alone is ~130 pieces with its interior) had their positions recomputed inside every render call, 4–6 times a frame (each view, the depth pre-pass, the shadow pass, mirrors). Now it happens once a frame
  • The quality governor now aims for 30 FPS instead of 60. It used to keep lowering the resolution and effects to chase 60, which is part of why the frame rate crept up the longer you played; now it only steps in below 30, so the graphics stay at full quality as long as the game holds 30

v19.5 · Nov 26, 2026 — fewer draw calls, self-tuning

  • Draw calls were the bottleneck: with a town in view the game was sending thousands of separate parts to the graphics card each frame, about half of them parked cars (each ~25 parts), plus crates, beds, manholes and signs. Past 16 m each of these is now drawn as one merged piece per material: the very same triangles, colours and textures, so it looks identical, at a fraction of the draw calls. Within 13 m, or as soon as a car moves or is used, the real parts return; stand-ins are refreshed every 8 s so damage and blood show on them
  • Self-tuning: the depth pre-pass and hiding things behind walls help some machines and cost others. Every 45 s of play the game briefly measures each combination and keeps the fastest. Neither changes the picture
  • Developer FPS counter now shows the average over the last minute under the current FPS; the perf overlay shows what the self-tuning picked and the frame times it measured

v19.4 · Nov 26, 2026 — every pixel lit once

  • Depth pre-pass: blocks and furniture are first drawn into the depth buffer only, so the expensive lighting (sun shadows, ray-traced shadows on Ultra, 12 lights, the flashlight) then runs once per screen pixel for the surface you actually see, instead of for every wall, floor and box behind it that got drawn first. No change to the picture
  • The sun’s shadow map is redrawn far less often: moving things only count within 32 m and when they’ve moved 10 cm or an eighth of a stride (it was every 5 cm and every frame of a walk out to 46 m, so any walking zombie redrew it every other frame)

v19.3 · Nov 26, 2026 — lighter hordes and details

  • Zombies past 30 m update their body pose every third frame instead of every frame (their AI still runs every frame); past 24 m they stop casting shadows
  • Grass is drawn out to 40 m and casts shadows within 24 m; blood marks and other decals are drawn within 34 m

v19.2 · Nov 26, 2026 — FPS counter

  • Turning on developer mode puts a framerate counter in the top-right corner, updated twice a second and shown even with menus open: green at 50 FPS and up, yellow from 30, red below

v19.1 · Nov 26, 2026 — fewer chunks drawn

  • The far view re-centres a little sooner as you travel

v19 · Nov 26, 2026 — faster, same picture

  • Every material is prepared while the world loads, so nothing stutters the first time it comes into view
  • Chunks are built against a time budget as well as a count: more per frame when there’s room, never enough to stall a frame
  • Covered chunks are re-checked every frame so they reappear at once; chunks already in view are re-checked every third frame, cutting the hidden-object tests by about two thirds
  • Props behind a covered chunk are found by a lookup instead of checking every prop against every chunk
  • The shadow pass no longer draws the sky, far view, particles and blood stains for nothing
  • Bookkeeping that scanned every chunk slot twice a frame, rebuilt the loaded-chunk map every frame and re-checked props ten times a second now only runs when a chunk comes or goes

v18.9 · Nov 26, 2026 — a better-looking picture

  • Vibrance lifts dull colours without making vivid ones garish
  • A gentle split tone: shadows a touch cooler, highlights a touch warmer
  • Phones and tablets now start on Medium graphics (shadows and post-processing on) instead of Low; the game still lowers its resolution by itself if frames drop, and Low is still in the settings

v18.8 · Nov 26, 2026 — no more see-through gaps

  • Fixed the gaps where you could see through the world. The cause was the blend at the edge of the detailed world: the near world and the far view each dropped a scattered half of their pixels there, and where the two surfaces sat at slightly different distances some pixels were dropped by both. The edge no longer cuts or blends: every loaded chunk is drawn in full, and the far view steps aside exactly on the chunks that are loaded, so every spot is drawn by one or the other
  • Hiding things behind walls now only ever counts the world that doesn’t move as cover, so a zombie or car passing in front can never make anything vanish, and its answers are checked every frame
  • The hidden-object check now leaves the renderer exactly as it found it, ruling out a way it could make other things draw wrong
  • The sky is drawn as before again
  • Props show wherever their chunk is loaded

v18.7 · Nov 26, 2026 — nothing hidden is drawn

  • Occlusion culling: after each frame, every chunk and every zombie, person, animal and parked car in view is tested against what was just drawn. Anything completely covered by something solid isn’t drawn the next frame: the chunk’s blocks, furniture, grass and glass, every prop inside it, and covered creatures and cars. Glass, water and other see-through things don’t count as cover, so what you can see through them is still drawn
  • Covered things still cast their shadows
  • An answer is only used while it still holds: you’ve moved less than 30 cm, nothing has been built, broken or opened, and if you’ve turned, only for things that were fully on screen. Anything without a fresh answer is drawn, so nothing you can see goes missing
  • The sky is now drawn last and only where nothing covers it; the far view and blood stains are drawn after the nearby world so covered pixels are skipped; stains and clouds out of view are skipped entirely
  • The developer “Render quality” lock is gone: the game always adapts its quality to the frame rate
  • The perf overlay shows how many chunks and creatures are hidden behind things

v18.6 · Nov 26, 2026 — nothing missing in view

  • Props (signs, crates, doors, beds, fires, traps, bags, blood marks, parked cars, manhole covers) are switched on a little before they reach the edge of the render distance, checked ten times a second instead of twice, and again the moment a chunk arrives, so nothing pops in late when you move or turn
  • Props are judged by where they actually are, not by their origin point, so nothing that’s on screen gets hidden by mistake
  • Grass is no longer cut off at the edge of the screen while it sways

v18.5 · Nov 26, 2026 — light the colour of the sky

  • Shade is lit by the sky: bluish on a clear day, pink-orange at dusk, cold blue under the moon, flat grey when overcast
  • Sunlit ground bounces warm light up onto the undersides of things
  • Ultra: sun shadows up close are twice as sharp (4096 map) on graphics cards that support it

v18.4 · Nov 26, 2026 — one round, steady render edge

  • The detailed world now ends at a true circle around you: the same distance in every direction, moving smoothly with you instead of jumping a chunk at a time
  • Chunks load two chunks beyond that circle and unload four beyond it, so loading and unloading always happens where nothing is drawn
  • Across the last 6 m the detailed world and the far view are dithered into each other pixel by pixel, so there is no hard line between them
  • If a chunk inside the circle isn’t ready yet (a very fast start or teleport), the far view keeps showing there instead of a hole

v18.3 · Nov 26, 2026 — developer render controls

  • Developer menu: Render quality — Adaptive (the game lowers quality to hold 60 fps) or Locked at full quality or any of the six lower steps. Locked means it never changes, even when frames drop; shading is still fine up close and coarse far away, by the same fixed amount every frame
  • Distance shading — Normal, Aggressive (coarser sooner), Gentle (fine for longer) or Off (full detail everywhere)
  • Show shading levels tints the world green (full detail) to red (coarsest), striped per level
  • The perf overlay shows the current quality step, whether it is locked, render scale and frame time. Both settings are remembered

v18.2 · Nov 26, 2026 — shading LOD

  • Shading and lighting now get coarser with distance, faster the further out you look: full detail within 6 m, then light and shadow are worked out on bigger and bigger pixels (1/16 m, 1/8, 1/4 … up to 4 m) as each extra metre counts for more. Each step fades into the next over its last 30%, so nothing pops or flickers as you move
  • Sun shadows: crisp five-sample shadows on every block pixel up close; single samples on coarser pixels at middle distance; past about 45 m a new far shadow map (four times the area, about 290 m across) averaged over each pixel, so small shadows melt into soft larger ones; very far, one sample per big pixel. Shadows now reach much further than before, and the far map is only redrawn when the sun or the blocks change
  • Hillside shading: at dawn and dusk, hills and ridges throw their shadows across valleys, the far view and the land around you alike, worked out from the far-view heights and blended over a few degrees as the sun sinks
  • Ray-traced block shadows take fewer steps the further out they are and hand over to the far shadow map; ray-traced ambient occlusion fades out past ~30 m; screen-space ambient occlusion uses fewer samples past 30 m and fades out from 150 to 220 m; puddle reflections take fewer steps further away
  • Lamps, headlights and the flashlight follow the same levels: worked out on the same bigger pixels further away, wet-ground glints dropped past middle distance
  • People, zombies and cars indoors are now shaded by the buildings around them even on Ultra (the far map includes the blocks)

v18.1 · Nov 26, 2026 — banded light, faster lighting

  • Every light (lamps, fires, street lamps, headlights, flares, flashes and the flashlight) now falls off in flat bands: small, gentle steps close to the light, wider bands and bigger drops further out, eight bands to the edge of its reach. Spotlights and the angle of a surface to the light step too
  • Light and sun shadow on people, zombies, cars and props are worked out at the centre of each of their own texture pixels, so lighting edges follow their pixels however they’re turned or moving. Blocks keep the block-pixel grid
  • Faster: lights that can’t reach anything on screen are dropped before the shader sees them; the sun’s shadow map is only redrawn when the sun, the shadow area, a block or something nearby has moved (at least twice a second); far lights skip the fine pass of their light maps
  • Developer perf overlay shows lights on screen, cached light maps and skipped shadow redraws

Pines take the high ground and grow in stands; birch groves and maples are scattered through the oak woods. Far-off trees show their own colours too

v13 · Nov 24, 2026 — pockets, wrecks and the open sky

  • Your pants are your pockets: how many pockets decides how many hotbar slots you get (shorts 2 up to cargo pants 8). You start with random pants.
  • Backpacks set your case size. Find them (schools are full of them), sew one, and upgrade it to a hiking pack or a military rucksack.
  • Hitting zombies splashes blood on the windshield and the part of the car that hit them. Cars dent, scuff, crack their glass and lose bumpers as they take damage. Hit something fast enough and you smash through walls, glass and furniture.
  • Bulldozers that push through anything, excavators that dig (hold attack), dump trucks, and the rare self-driving Volta: open the map (M) and click where to go.
  • Driver view: mostly windshield, with the top of the dash and a sliver of hood.
  • Creative mode repairs and refuels any vehicle for free.
  • The game tells you, and everyone online, how you died.
  • Cities have bigger blocks with office towers, plus colleges. Kitchens have plates, bowls, cups and cutlery. Bathroom mirrors show your reflection.
  • Country roads climb hills smoothly. Clouds drift overhead, flocks of birds cross the sky, and the wind carries far-off moans, banging doors and howling.
  • Everyone is pixel-textured: skin, woven shirts, denim. Cigarettes and marijuana can be found and smoked.

v12 · Nov 24, 2026 — the wider world

  • Winding roads between towns, graded into the hills, that run straight out of a town street at full width so the join is seamless, with bridges over rivers and ponds. Off-road dirt trails.

  • Hamlets, big cities (taller buildings, much more traffic), gas stations with pumps you can siphon, parks with benches, swings, slides and a fountain, malls with shops and a food court.

  • More realistic sound: footsteps, gunshots, engine, thunder, wind and rain.
  • Fixes: creative camera lock, road paint over holes, mobile controls hidden on computers.

Build
Zombies jump 2 blocks and smash wood, glass and doors — wall up 3 high in stone or metal
Cars
Need fuel cans; wrecks need a repair kit
Traps
Spikes wound and slow, bear traps pin a walker, tripwire bombs blow up a crowd (and you)
Sewers
Climb down open manholes in the roads. Dark, crawling with dead, better loot
Seasons
Spring, summer, autumn and winter turn every 3 days. Winter brings snow, short days and cold — stay indoors, by a fire, or wear a coat
Night
Sleep in a bed to skip to dawn (sets your respawn). In co-op both players must lie down
Caves
Openings in the hills lead down into dark tunnels with ore and stashes. Some houses have basements
Hunting
Deer and rabbits flee, wolves and boars fight back. Cook meat at a campfire; wear armor from crates, zombies and the workbench
People
Survivors live in the towns — they shoot zombies and trade. Bandits roam the wilds and shoot on sight
Phone / tablet
Drag left side to move, right side to look; on-screen buttons for the rest
