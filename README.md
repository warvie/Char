# Dead Ground

A 3D zombie-apocalypse survival game that runs in the browser. Raid a ruined town for loot, dig into the earth for coal and iron, build walls before nightfall, and get an old car running.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). It needs an internet connection the first time to load Three.js from cdnjs.

To play from a link, turn on GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**. The game will be at `https://warvie.github.io/char/`.

## Features

- Voxel world built from small half-size, pixel-textured blocks (grass, dirt, stone, ores, brick, planks, bark, corrugated metal, glass) that you can dig and build in. Stone starts about 7 blocks down, with coal and iron deeper.
- Ruined concrete and brick buildings with framed glass windows (some smashed or boarded up), front and back doors that open and close, stairs, and supply crates to raid.
- Sewers under every road: climb down open manholes, wade past the sludge channel in the dark, and find better-stocked stashes guarded by the dead.
- Zombies that are slow by day and fast at night. They jump 2 blocks and smash wood, glass and doors, so build walls 3 high in stone or metal.
- Traps: spike traps (wound and slow), bear traps (pin a walker in place) and tripwire bombs (blow up a crowd, and you, if you're close).
- Nail bat, pistol (headshots kill in one hit) and pickaxe.
- Crafting: planks, metal plates, ammo, bandages, repair kits and makeshift fuel.
- Drivable sedans and pickup trucks that need fuel and repairs. Drive from a first-person seat with a turning steering wheel, working speed and fuel gauges, warning lights, mirror and seats, or switch to the outside view. Run zombies over, carry a passenger, and use the headlights at night.
- Health, hunger, stamina, day/night cycle and flashlight.
- 2-player split-screen co-op on one computer (keyboard + mouse, and keyboard or gamepad).
- Phone and tablet support with a virtual joystick, drag-to-look and on-screen buttons.

## Controls

**Player 1:** WASD move · mouse look · click act · right-click place block · 1–5 / wheel tools (bat, pistol, pickaxe, build, trap) · R block material or trap type · E use / doors / enter car · V driver or outside view · Space jump (brake in car) · Shift sprint · Tab backpack · G eat · X bandage · T flashlight

**Player 2:** I K / J L move & turn · U / O look · H act · Y use · N jump · P sprint · B tool · M material or trap type · , car view · [ eat · ] bandage · \ backpack · or a gamepad

**Touch:** drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look · Act, Jump, Use, Place buttons bottom right · Bag, Tool, Type, View, Run along the top
